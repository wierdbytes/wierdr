At a glance

Every agent.
One list.

All your spaces, tabs and agents — on your Mac and every machine it's connected to. Color-coded by status, with the model, reasoning effort, context and sub-agents right there.

  • Needs you, done, working, idle — color-coded
  • Sub-agents and teammates in a live tree
  • One tap takes you to whoever needs you next

Real terminals

Reply right in the terminal.

Not a chat imitation — the agent's real, live terminal. Approve a command with one tap, answer a question, send the next prompt.

  • A full terminal: Claude Code, Codex, pi, vim and btop just work
  • A key panel for Esc, arrows, Tab and Return
  • Type, dictate, paste a screenshot or send a photo

Live Activity

Your agents, on your Lock Screen.

A Live Activity counts your agents by status and shows the most urgent ones — even with Wierdr closed. Your screen lights up the moment an agent needs you. And yes, it lives in the Dynamic Island too.

  • Live updates, even with the app closed
  • Tap an agent to jump straight to it
  • Agent names stay encrypted between your Mac and iPhone

Private by design.

Wierdr talks to your Mac directly over SSH. No cloud between you and your terminals, no account, no tracking.

  • Direct SSHYour terminals travel only between your device and your Mac.
  • Keys stay on your deviceThe SSH key is made on your iPhone or iPad and lives in its Keychain.
  • Sealed notificationsAgent names on your Lock Screen are encrypted with a key only your devices share.
  • No account. No analytics.Nothing to sign up for, nothing watching you.

How it works

Up and running in minutes.

  1. 1

    Install Herdr on your Mac

    Herdr is where your coding agents live. Turn on Remote Login, and your Mac is ready.

  2. 2

    Add your Mac in Wierdr

    Enter its address — at home, or anywhere with Tailscale. Wierdr offers to set up the rest.

  3. 3

    Go anywhere

    Reply from your iPhone or iPad. Your iPhone's Lock Screen shows who needs you. Answer in seconds.
